# How Dump Sites Get Questions & Our Strategy

## How Dump Sites Source Questions:

### 1. **Exam Takers Share Questions** (Primary Source)
- People take real exams (AWS, CISSP, etc.)
- Memorize questions and share them online
- Dump sites collect and compile these
- **Legal Risk**: Violates exam NDAs and copyrights

### 2. **Practice Test Providers**
- Some legitimate practice test companies
- Questions leak from their databases
- Former employees share question banks

### 3. **Community Contributions**
- Forums like Reddit, Discord, Telegram groups
- People share "brain dumps" after exams
- Crowdsourced question collection

### 4. **Reverse Engineering**
- Analyze official study guides
- Create similar questions based on exam objectives
- Use AI to generate variations

## Our Legal & Ethical Approach:

### ✅ What We CAN Do:
1. **Create Original Questions** based on public documentation
2. **Use Official Exam Objectives** to guide question creation
3. **Reference Public Knowledge** (AWS docs, OWASP guides)
4. **AI-Generated Questions** based on legitimate sources
5. **Community-Contributed** original questions

### ❌ What We CANNOT Do:
1. Copy actual exam questions (copyright violation)
2. Use "brain dumps" from exam takers
3. Scrape copyrighted content from other sites
4. Violate exam provider NDAs

## Our Strategy - Better Than Dumps:

### 1. **Comprehensive Question Database**
- 500+ original questions per certification
- Based on official exam objectives
- Updated with latest technology trends

### 2. **AI-Powered Question Generation**
- Generate unlimited practice questions
- Based on official documentation
- Adaptive difficulty based on user performance

### 3. **Real-World Scenarios**
- Questions based on actual job requirements
- Practical scenarios vs memorization
- Industry best practices focus
